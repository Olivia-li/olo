# Olo Color Experience

## New Color Just Dropped

[OloNewColor.com](https://www.olonewcolor.com) is an interactive experiment that simulates "olo" - a newly discovered color that normally requires specialized laboratory equipment to perceive.

## What is Olo?

Researchers led by Fong et al. recently discovered a new color perception by selectively stimulating individual M-cone photoreceptors using adaptive optics and precise lasers. This color - dubbed "olo" - exists beyond the normal human color gamut.

## How This Simulation Works

While your computer screen can't directly produce olo, this experiment creates a similar perceptual effect by:

1. **Cone Fatigue**: Exposing your eyes to pure red light (≈630nm) for 25 seconds to temporarily reduce L-cone sensitivity
2. **M-Cone Stimulation**: Briefly flashing green light (≈530nm) for 1 second to primarily stimulate M-cones
3. **Unique Perception**: The resulting color perception - often described as a vivid teal or blue-green - hints at the olo corner of color space

## Instructions

1. Visit [OloNewColor.com](https://www.olonewcolor.com)
2. For best results:
   - Maximize screen brightness
   - View in a dimly lit room
   - Focus your gaze on the "+" during the entire red phase
3. Click "Start" and maintain focus for the full 25-second red phase
4. Notice the unique color that appears during the brief green flash
5. The color fades quickly as your L-cones recover their sensitivity

## About the Science

This simulation leverages principles of human color perception and cone adaptation. By selectively reducing L-cone sensitivity through chromatic adaptation while leaving M-cones relatively fresh, we can temporarily shift perception toward colors that emphasize M-cone stimulation.
